## Exit survey

**Currently disabled (2026-10-04, owner request):** the `{% render %}` in `sections/belle-footer.liquid` is commented out. Uncomment it to restore.

`snippets/belle-exit-survey.liquid` (added 2026-09-24, rendered from `belle-footer.liquid` next to `calendly-modal`): a short "why are you leaving" popup. Desktop fires on classic exit-intent (cursor leaves the top of the viewport); touch layouts have no cursor to leave with, so they use a best-effort heuristic instead (a fast upward scroll back near the top of the page after having scrolled down), which will both miss real exits and occasionally false-positive. At most once per tab (`sessionStorage`), and skipped while the Calendly modal is open. It publishes `exit_survey_shown` / `exit_survey_response` / `exit_survey_dismissed` via `Shopify.analytics.publish`, which reach GA4 through a **new, separate** Shopify custom pixel documented in `docs/ga4-exit-survey-pixel.js` - do not confuse this with the removed `docs/ga4-custom-pixel.js` below, and do not merge the two pixels. None of these three events are conversions; don't mark them as GA4 key events.

On a chosen reason, it also emails the response via **EmailJS** (client-side send, no backend) to sales@bellesperformancetennis.com. **Do not try routing this through Shopify's native `{% form 'contact' %}` instead** - that was tried and confirmed broken 2026-09-24: a silent/background submission gets rejected with `"Missing CAPTCHA token"`, because Shopify's contact form requires a token that only its own bot-protection script can generate, which only runs against a genuinely visible, interacted-with form. EmailJS is set up and live: `EMAILJS_SERVICE_ID` (`service_kn1nejo`, Gmail service connected as sales@bellesperformancetennis.com) and `EMAILJS_TEMPLATE_ID` (`template_o02snl7`, the "Contact Us" template, To Email fixed to that same address) near the top of the snippet's `<script>` are real values, verified with a live 200/OK send 2026-09-24, not placeholders. The EmailJS account itself, its templates, and the connected Gmail live outside this repo - if the send ever needs debugging, check the EmailJS dashboard (emailjs.com), not just the theme code. Don't repoint `EMAILJS_TEMPLATE_ID` at the account's other template, "Auto-Reply" (`template_2lr0lnf`) - that one is meant to reply to a form submitter's own address, and this survey never collects a visitor email.

After picking a reason, the visitor lands on an optional "want me to follow up?" step (name, email, zip code, all optional, one "Send" button that submits whatever was filled in) before the response actually goes out - added so the fast one-click path still exists but people willing to leave contact info can. Those three fields go **only** to the EmailJS send, never to the GA4 pixel (`track()` only ever sends `reason`/`reason_detail`/`page_path` - see the comment directly above `respond()` in the snippet; Google's terms prohibit sending PII to GA4, and there's no reporting need for it there anyway). The EmailJS template needs `{{name}}`, `{{email}}`, and `{{zip}}` placeholders in its body for these to actually show up in the email; if the template's ever rebuilt, carry those over from `template_o02snl7`.
